# Lyte of Utopia RPG

_Lyte of Utopia_ is a text RPG based on the web novel of the same name by DNA Studios.

You play as **Akira Lyte** and follow his story as it's told in the novel, but with the added twist of being able to change the course of events as much or as little as you like. Explore the world and uncover the secrets hidden in it, or head straight through the main story. It's all up to you.

# Installation

After downloading the zip file, extract it and then open the index.html file, it'll open the game in your preferred web browser.

Works best in Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

**Game will not save in incognito windows, so once you close out the window your save data will be gone forever**
