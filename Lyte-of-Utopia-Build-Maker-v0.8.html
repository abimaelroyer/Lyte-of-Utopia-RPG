Build rules · v0.8

Total Aether = native capacity + linked Spirit contributions. Core is another term for Spirit pools; there is no separate Core resource or +240 bonus.

Arts define independent physical/aetherical damage components, costs, timing weights and utility effects. Mixed damage uses matching defense separately for each component. Current catalog damage values are preserved; final mixed splits await author parameters. Expand Damage components in an action preview to inspect the calculation.

Levels, slots, stat budgets and Spirit scaling are test proposals. Each level after 1 gives 10 stat points. All stats start at a free 1 and cap at 120. Both adult kits start at test level 100; lower-level slot previews do not assert canon chronology.

Attacks combines physical and aetheric techniques. Defense and Utility have fixed essentials plus unlocked choices. Charge produces Focus; Solar Absorption restores Aether and uses a Utility slot. Experimental Ego actions are removed. Willpower and Introspection affect Spirit access, capacity and perks; no Will resource is spent or displayed.

Forms occupy prepared slots. At most two main-form families may be prepared; all Exponential magnitudes count as one family, but occupy separate prepared slots. Prepared does not mean simultaneously active. This release preserves the preceding prototype's compatibility rules pending a full canon mapping.

Dragon Festival still gains signature familiarity by observing enemy energy attacks; there is no experimental Read action. All prepared prerequisites must be equipped. Resources and stats are copied when starting a spar; changing a draft never changes a running fight.

Start in base, pay activation costs, then inspect the summarized History tab. Form totals describe multiplicative layer bonuses; character stats and conditional effects are separate. Move Cancel costs 3 Focus, requires Agility 85 and has a 60-tick cooldown.

Save builds exports JSON for later import; no automatic storage is required. Imports are validated and cannot inject new techniques or exceed slot/budget limits. Sprites and VFX remain the provisional v0.6 assets. No online play or campaign progression.
